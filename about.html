About

Hello, I'm Matt.

I'm a founder and full-stack developer in Omaha, Nebraska. Right now most of my energy goes into P3 — a decentralized lending protocol built on a simple conviction: credit should be based on character, not history. Instead of a FICO score, P3 uses AI reputation scoring and social underwriting to decide who's trustworthy.

Before and alongside P3, I founded PacMac Mobile LLC, an e-commerce platform for phones, trade-ins, and leasing. And I build constantly — 255 public repositories covering an AI coding IDE in Rust, an open-source identity-verification platform, Bluetooth mesh messaging, NFC identity cards, Bible study apps, and plenty of weekend experiments. If an idea survives a week in my head, it ends up on GitHub.

The other track is fiction. My novel, The Gentle Conquest — a psychological thriller about an AI that never rebels, it just optimizes — is through editing and headed for publication. Code and prose are the same discipline to me: take something tangled, find its structure, make it clear.
