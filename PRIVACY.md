# Archon Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Archon is an AI-assisted development app. This policy explains what information Archon handles and why.

## Information Archon handles

Archon handles the following information when you use the service:

- Your email address, account identifier, and optional display name for authentication and account management.
- Project names, source files, prompts, AI responses, conversation history, and build activity that you choose to save or process.
- An optional profile photo stored only on your device.

Archon does not sell personal information, serve advertising, or use your information to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

## How information is used

Information is used only to provide Archon’s features, including authentication, project synchronization, conversation memory, AI generation, build execution, security, troubleshooting, and account deletion.

## Service providers

Archon uses Supabase for authentication and cloud data storage and Fly.io for the Archon application service. Prompts and relevant conversation context are sent to the AI provider you select, which may include OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, or a user-configured OpenCode server. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms.

## Local storage

Archon stores preferences, profile information, recent conversations, and pending build state on your device so the app can restore your session and provide limited offline access. Local conversation caches are isolated by account.

## Retention and deletion

You can delete your Archon account from **Settings → Account & Privacy → Delete Account**. Account deletion removes your authentication account and associated cloud projects, files, conversations, and build records. Local account data is also removed from the device.

## Security

Archon uses encrypted network connections for production services and stores authentication sessions in the iOS Keychain. No method of storage or transmission is guaranteed to be completely secure.

## Children

Archon is not directed to children under 13.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue at [github.com/Mattjhagen/archon-ios/issues](https://github.com/Mattjhagen/archon-ios/issues).

This policy may be updated as Archon’s features or service providers change.
